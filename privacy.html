Summary

Flag Guesser is a free browser game. Solo play requires no account and sends no personal information anywhere. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen when you use the site.

Solo play

When you play alone, everything happens entirely inside your own browser. No game data, scores, or settings are sent to any server or stored anywhere outside your device.

Versus mode

Versus mode connects your browser directly to another player's browser (a peer-to-peer connection) so you can play together in real time. To make that connection possible:

  • A connection-brokering service (PeerJS) briefly helps the two browsers find each other. It only relays the technical information needed to establish a connection, not your gameplay.
  • If a direct connection isn't possible (for example, if you and your opponent are on very different networks), a relay service (Cloudflare) may pass encrypted connection traffic between you. This service does not read or store the content of what's sent.
  • The only information exchanged between players is what's needed to play: the current flag, your answers, and scores. Whatever name you type in before a versus match is shared with your opponent for that match only and is not stored anywhere afterward.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and does not build any profile of visitors.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes, in accordance with GitHub's own privacy statement.

Advertising

If this site displays advertising, ads are served through a third-party ad network, which may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads, in accordance with that network's own privacy policy. Where required, this page will be updated to reflect the specific ad network in use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a revised date above.
