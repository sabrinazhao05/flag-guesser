The basics

Each round, a flag appears and you have to name its country before the timer runs out. You can answer two ways: pick from four multiple-choice options, or type the country's name directly (small typos are forgiven, but mixing up two genuinely different countries — like Iran and Iraq — is still marked wrong).

Answer mode

Multiple choice gives you four options, one correct. Type answer asks you to type the name yourself and press Enter to lock it in.

Timer

Choose 3, 5, or 10 seconds per flag. This applies no matter which length or difficulty you've selected.

Length

  • 10 / 25 / 50 flags — a fixed-length round. You play through misses and see your final accuracy at the end.
  • All flags — every flag in your chosen difficulty tier. In Typing or Multiple Choice mode, one wrong answer ends the run immediately, so it plays like a genuine challenge run rather than a scored quiz.
  • Infinite — flags keep coming, one after another, until you miss one or the timer runs out. Your streak is the score.

Difficulty

  • Easy — around 45 widely recognized countries, with multiple-choice wrong answers pulled from different continents so they look visually distinct.
  • Medium — around 86 countries, a mix of familiar and moderately known flags.
  • Hard — all 195 countries, with multiple-choice wrong answers weighted toward the same continent, so you're often choosing between genuinely similar-looking flags.

Versus mode

Create a room to get a 4-digit PIN, or join a friend's PIN from another device — anywhere, not just the same WiFi. Once you're both in, the host starts the match and you'll see the exact same flag at the exact same moment. Whoever answers correctly first scores the point; a wrong guess hands the point straight to your opponent. Play Best of 5, Best of 10, or Infinite, and request a rematch when you're done — both players have to agree before a new match starts.

Pause button

Flip on the pause button setting (bottom-left corner) if you'd like a pause option during solo games. When it's on, a small button appears on the flag box mid-round — press it to freeze the timer exactly where it is, and press it again (or hit Escape) to pick up right where you left off.
